      Services and what can be received

      DSC (VHF Ch 70, MF/HF)
      Distress, urgency and safety calls, from a DSC radio's NMEA output or an SDR decoder at the station.
      AIS-SART, MOB, EPIRB-AIS
      Seen directly on AIS and over internet AIS. Shown in red and flashing.
      EPIRB / PLB 406 MHz
      Local bursts decoded by the station's receiver: beacon ID, type and country. Alerts are relayed to rescue centres by COSPAS-SARSAT, not by Marine Monitor.
      NAVTEX 518 / 490 kHz
      Navigational and weather warnings, received at the station.
      Inmarsat-C SafetyNET
      Public maritime safety broadcasts (EGC), from an L-band receiver. Private Inmarsat traffic is not received or decoded.
      SSAS (ship security alert)
      Not receivable, by design. SSAS is covert and goes only to the ship's company and flag state. Marine Monitor doesn't try to detect or intercept it.

      MMSI decoder

      DSC call: individual or group

      Calls are sent by the station's type-approved DSC radio (Uniden UM435). Marine Monitor prepares the call, shows the exact DSC sequence, logs it and watches for the acknowledgement. It never transmits.

            Other receivers (optional)

              Connecting the radios

              1. UM435 (Band A): NMEA OUT wires → USB-to-NMEA/RS-422 adapter → station PC. Program the station's MMSI in the UM435.
              2. IC-9100 (Band B): one USB cable carries CI-V control and receive audio. Menu: CI-V USB Echo Back OFF, CI-V baud 19200, USB audio output level about 50%. Marine Monitor tunes the main receiver and decodes AIS and DSC from the audio in software.
              3. TH-D74 (Band C): micro-USB cable to the station PC. Menu 980 USB Function = COM+AF/IF Output, Menu 102 Detect Out = Detect (flat FM detector audio, best for AIS; use Off (AF) for HF DSC), Menu 918 USB audio level about 5, squelch open. Marine Monitor tunes Band B over the USB COM port.
              4. Run python helper/marine_helper.py --serial COM3 --baud 4800 --rig ic9100 --civ-port COM4 --mode ais-a --d74-port COM5 --d74-audio "TH-D74" --d74-mode ais-b and put its address in Settings → Station receiver. Add --relay and --feed-key so phones anywhere see the station.

              Each radio hears one frequency at a time. With both, put the IC-9100 on AIS 1 and the TH-D74 on AIS 2 so both AIS channels are heard all the time (the UM435 keeps the DSC watch). With one radio, "AIS scan" alternates Ch 87B and 88B. Receive only: Marine Monitor never transmits.
