Distress, urgency and safety (Sécurité)

    NAVAREA IV navigational warnings (NGA, internet)

      Services and what can be received

      DSC (VHF Ch 70, MF/HF)
      Distress, urgency and safety calls, from a DSC radio's NMEA output or an SDR decoder at the station.
      AIS-SART, MOB, EPIRB-AIS
      Seen directly on AIS and over internet AIS. Shown in red and flashing.
      EPIRB / PLB 406 MHz
      Local bursts decoded by the station's receiver: beacon ID, type and country. Alerts are relayed to rescue centres by COSPAS-SARSAT, not by Marine Monitor.
      NAVTEX 518 / 490 kHz
      Navigational and weather warnings, received at the station.
      Inmarsat-C SafetyNET
      Public maritime safety broadcasts (EGC), from an L-band receiver. Private Inmarsat traffic is not received or decoded.
      SSAS (ship security alert)
      Not receivable, by design. SSAS is covert and goes only to the ship's company and flag state. Marine Monitor doesn't try to detect or intercept it.

      Tide (sea level, next 36 h)

      Observations

      Model tides and currents are about 8 km resolution: not for navigation. Use official tide tables and the Barbados Meteorological Services marine forecast.

          Setting up direct RF

          1. At the station, connect a receiver to a PC or Raspberry Pi: an RTL-SDR (AIS, DSC, 406 MHz; NAVTEX needs HF coverage), or a marine VHF/AIS unit with NMEA output.
          2. Run helper/marine_helper.py. It takes NMEA from AIS-catcher, rtl_ais, a DSC radio or a NAVTEX/EGC decoder, and sends it to this app and to the relay.
          3. Set the receiver address below, or set FEED_KEY so phones anywhere see the station's RF picture through the relay.

          Receive only. Marine Monitor never transmits.
